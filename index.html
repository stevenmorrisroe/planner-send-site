crew planner send

crew planner send is a private, single-user tool. It sends emails that its owner has already written and approved in his own planner. When he taps "Send it" on a draft, the planner sends that exact email from his own Gmail account.

It asks Google for one permission only: gmail.send. It can send mail, and it cannot read, search, change or delete any email.

The app is not offered to anyone else, and it has no sign-up.
